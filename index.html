How it works

From camera to case in three steps

STEP 1

Connect

Point your existing cameras at Watchline over ONVIF or RTSP. Most sites are live within a day, no new wiring.

STEP 2

Understand

The vision-language model indexes every frame — people, vehicles, actions, and context — as it happens, not after a request.

STEP 3

Act

Verified events trigger the right response automatically: an alert, a deterrent, or a case — while a human stays in control of every action.
